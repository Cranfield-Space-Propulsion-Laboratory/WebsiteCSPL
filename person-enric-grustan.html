Dr Enric Grustan-Gutiérrez

Principal Investigator

Lecturer in Space Engineering · Centre for Autonomous and Cyber-Physical Systems

I joined Cranfield University in 2022 after three years as a Lecturer in Aerospace Propulsion at the University of Glasgow. At Cranfield, in addition to my research, I am the module director for Space Propulsion and Space Electric Propulsion.

Previously at Queen Mary University of London, I developed electrospray thrusters for the European Space Agency. Before that I spent a year at Soochow University working on 2D materials and microelectronics.

I hold a PhD in Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering from the University of California, Irvine, awarded in 2015 for work on multiplexed electrospray sources for propulsion and physical sputtering, and a double master's in Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering from BarcelonaTech and the University of Padua.

Currently, my research has branched into different fields of in-space propulsion, with a focus on electric propulsion (and, obviously, electrosprays).
