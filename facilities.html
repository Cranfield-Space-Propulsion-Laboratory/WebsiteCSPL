Home / Facilities

Built and tested on Cranfield's own campus.

Three spaces, from vacuum to clean-room, covering the full range of CSPL's propulsion and small-satellite work.

Facility 01

Space Environment Facility

CSPL's vacuum chamber facility replicates the high-vacuum, low-pressure conditions of space, allowing electric and electrospray thrusters to be fired and characterised under representative operating conditions. The facility supports thrust and beam diagnostics, propellant feed testing, and long-duration life testing for small-satellite propulsion hardware.

Facility 02

Rocket Propulsion Laboratory

A dedicated, safety-rated test warehouse for chemical propulsion hot-fire testing — from the student-built Cyclone liquid rocket engine to rotating detonation engine rigs and hydrogen storage trials. The lab also houses the group's collection of student and amateur rocketry hardware.

Facility 03

Clean Room

A 4m × 4m Class 100,000 clean room, with zones down to Class 10,000, used for assembling small satellites and payloads. It has been used to build de-orbit sails now in orbit, as well as other space flight hardware projects across the university.
